Privacy Policy

Type Again on Mac

Updated 10 October 2026

No Data Collected

Type Again has no server, requires no account, and requests no network access. Your text, progress, display name, and settings all stay on your device. When you import, create, or edit files, Type Again reads and writes them directly in the macOS file system; we never receive a copy. The same goes for shareable cards — Type Again hands them to the macOS share sheet, and you choose what happens from there; we do not receive a copy either.

No Third-Party Sharing

Type Again does not use any analytics tool, advertising network or third-party SDK. We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not collect any in the first place.

Already All Yours

Since Type Again does not collect personal data or request consent for such collection, there is no server-side data to retain or delete, and no consent to revoke — your device already holds everything, under your control, including a Factory Reset button to wipe it clean whenever you like.
